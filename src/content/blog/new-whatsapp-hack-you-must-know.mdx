A "friend" sends you a message: *"Sorry, I sent you a code by mistake, can you send it back?"* Ma tsiftlo walo! That code is your WhatsApp verification code.

## How it works
- The scammer tries to register **your number** on their phone.
- WhatsApp sends **you** the 6-digit code.
- You forward it, and they take over your account and message your contacts asking for money.

## Lock it down
- **Settings → Account → Two-step verification.** Set a PIN now.
- Never share any code, even with "family". Call them to check.
- Add an email address so you can recover your account.
