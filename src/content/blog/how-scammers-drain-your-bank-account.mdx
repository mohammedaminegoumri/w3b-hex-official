It starts with one message: *"Your account has been suspended, click here."* Wakha tbanlik normal, this is how most account-draining scams begin.

## The playbook
1. **The hook.** An SMS, call or email pretending to be your bank, a delivery company or a government office.
2. **The panic.** "Act in 10 minutes or lose your money." Urgency stops you from thinking.
3. **The grab.** A fake login page or a request for your **OTP code**. With that code, they're in.
4. **The drain.** Transfers, card payments, and new devices linked to your account. All fast.

## Protect yourself
- Your bank will **never** ask for your password or OTP. Never, 3mrha.
- Type your bank's address yourself. Don't click links in messages.
- Turn on transaction alerts and app-based 2FA.
- If something feels off, hang up and call the official number on your card.

> Got hit? Call your bank right away, block the card, and report it. Speed matters.
