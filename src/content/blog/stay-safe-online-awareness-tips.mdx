Not everything online is what it looks like, especially now that AI can fake voices, faces and whole websites. Here are the basics that work.

## 10 habits
1. Use a password manager with unique passwords.
2. Turn on 2FA everywhere, preferably with an app rather than SMS.
3. Update your phone and apps.
4. Check the URL before you log in.
5. "Too good to be true" usually is (free iPhones, crypto that doubles your money).
6. Verify viral news from two real sources before you share it.
7. Lock down your social media privacy settings.
8. Don't install APKs from random links.
9. Be careful with public Wi-Fi.
10. Talk to your parents about scams. They're the main targets.
